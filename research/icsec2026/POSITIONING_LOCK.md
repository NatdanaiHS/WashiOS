# 1. Research gap

Among the closest published studies reviewed, Martínez et al. report effective-injection confirmation and invalid-sequence filtering for pin-level fault injection, and Batista et al. report scripted communication-bus injection with observation around both communicating subsystems, but we did not find in the cited papers a published payload-link trial protocol that jointly makes controller detection scoreable only after separate payload-side observation/confirmation of the realized fault condition and recovery scoreable only after separate payload-side confirmation of NORMAL restoration (verified sources [A]-[G] in Section 5).

# 2. Proposed solution

We propose a confirmation-gated experimental protocol for auditable HIL fault-injection trials centered on two score-eligibility gates—payload-side confirmation before detection scoring and payload-side NORMAL confirmation before recovery scoring—operationalized through distinct requested, activated, detected, restored, and recovered events and supported by byte-preserving endpoint traces and hash-verified frozen summaries.

# 3. Bounded contributions in priority order

1. A two-stage confirmation-gated trial protocol that treats an injection request only as a request, requires separate payload-side observation/confirmation before controller detection is scoreable, and requires separate payload-side confirmation of NORMAL before controller recovery is scoreable.
2. An explicit operationalization of those gates through distinct requested, activated, detected, restored, and recovered events, with controller- and payload-side host timestamps, exact received bytes, readable renderings, and SHA-256 verification supporting auditability rather than constituting an independent novelty claim.
3. A setup-bounded descriptive evaluation of the protocol using one G431/G474 pair for the primary 90-trial campaign, a separate 605-s nominal observation, two BAD_CRC mechanism observations, and two separate 12-row F411 pair campaigns whose denominators and interpretations remain separate.

# 4. This paper does NOT claim

- a WashiOS architecture contribution;
- a new CubeSat HIL framework, fault injector, payload simulator, or fault model;
- global novelty or comparative advantage over the literature;
- a reliability estimate, device-population inference, environmental robustness result, flight qualification, mission assurance, or complete-system validation;
- independent hardware replication of the primary G431/G474 setup, pooling of sequential trials or F411 pairs, or MCU-family equivalence;
- controller-internal timing from host serial timestamps;
- a comparative baseline or C0 ablation;
- that missing a restart marker proves that no reset occurred; or
- that the evaluated endpoint conditions are equivalent to electrical, environmental, or radiation-induced faults.

# 6. Exact role of each retained campaign component in demonstrating the method

- **Primary G431/G474 campaign:** This is the main end-to-end demonstration of the scoring protocol on one physical controller/payload pair: 90 sequential trials, 30 each for SILENT, BAD_CRC, and DELAYED, with 90 confirmed activations, 90 confirmed NORMAL restorations, and the predefined detector and recovery markers observed in 30/30 valid trials per mode; it demonstrates operability and traceability of the full requested-to-recovered chain on the evaluated setup, not reliability or population generality. Authoritative repository evidence: `runs/full_20260830_seed20260830_n30/{run_plan.csv,results.csv,validation.json,summary.json,SHA256SUMS.csv}`, `paper/RESULTS.md`, and the exact-byte recovery record `extension/recovery/recovery_20260901/RECOVERY_RECORD.json`.
- **605-s nominal observation:** This is a separate duration-bounded check of the protocol’s healthy-state observation boundary, with 121 ONLINE status records, strictly increasing `ok` from 1760 to 2960, zero timeout/CRC/sequence/recovery counter deltas, and no prohibited marker; it is neither a primary fault trial nor a reliability estimate. Authoritative repository evidence: `extension/evidence/primary_20260830_seed20260830_b5/nominal_validation_002.json`, frozen SHA-256 `3119D5994378E00C7ACE945B0FCB96CBA28C5855CDFEA445CD26570FD52A74FD`.
- **BAD_CRC characterization:** The SHORT and SUSTAINED observations explain why CRC rejection is the BAD_CRC detector while OFFLINE remains secondary: SHORT records CRC rejection followed by one timeout and no OFFLINE before confirmed restoration, whereas SUSTAINED records CRC rejection, timeout counts one and two, then OFFLINE at count three before confirmed restoration and recovery; these are two mechanism observations, not additions to the 90-trial denominator. Authoritative repository evidence: `extension/evidence/primary_20260830_seed20260830_b5/bad_crc_results.json` and its four endpoint logs, frozen result SHA-256 `F6332F913FFA91432032AA1E5582AE78378F8880FC22D11B921C5B793ADC4E90`.
- **F411 two-pair extension:** This is a separate protocol-level, cross-platform extension using the same fixed F411 implementation and the same predefined 12-row protocol on each of two physical pairs; each pair separately retained 12/12 valid rows, clean 3/3 normal controls, accepted 90-ms delayed responses in 3/3 without fault transitions, and showed timeout/sequence-rejection/OFFLINE followed by confirmed restoration and recovery in 3/3 at both 100 and 110 ms, supporting pair-specific portability of the confirmation gates without pooling, G431/G474 timing replication, MCU equivalence, or population inference. Authoritative repository evidence: the two `f411_pair*_campaign_20260901_seed20260901_b3` packages and `extension/analysis/f411_cross_pair_synthesis_20260902_r4/{SYNTHESIS.md,PROVENANCE.json}`.

# 7. Proposed final paper title

**Confirmation-Gated HIL Fault-Injection Trials for Auditable Payload-Link Supervision**

# 8. Six-page story outline

1. **Page 1 - Problem and claim boundary:** Frame the evidentiary problem—an injection request does not prove endpoint activation—and state the one-sentence gap, solution, exactly three bounded contributions, and the non-architecture/non-framework/non-reliability scope.
2. **Page 2 - Closest literature and system context:** Position FARM, AFIT, injection-fidelity work, Abboush HIL, Batista/FEM, and Conceição/SATS; reduce WashiOS/G431/G474 to the minimum payload-link supervision context needed to define silence, bad CRC, delay, detector markers, and recovery markers.
3. **Page 3 - Confirmation-gated protocol and audit model:** Define the requested → activated → detected → restored → recovered event sequence, separate payload-side and controller-side observation channels on the same host, validity gates, detector rules, recovery rule, host-timestamp limitation, exact-byte trace format, SHA-256 freeze, and deterministic table projection.
4. **Page 4 - Primary evidence:** Report the two 65-s primary N0 windows, the 90 sequential G431/G474 trials with per-mode denominators and descriptive host-observed latencies, the separate 605-s nominal observation, and the SHORT/SUSTAINED BAD_CRC mechanism ordering without converting any count into reliability.
5. **Page 5 - Bounded extension and validity:** Report each F411 pair as its own 12-row dataset, explain what the repeated condition-level pattern says about protocol-level portability, and present construct, internal, external, timing, hardware-dependence, and fault-model limitations.
6. **Page 6 - Discussion, reproducibility, and conclusion:** State what confirmation gating changes in the interpretation of HIL trials, connect every quantitative statement to byte-preserving traces and hash-verified summaries, restate the conservative literature position and prohibited inferences, conclude with the setup-bounded methodological result, and fit the verified references within the page budget.
